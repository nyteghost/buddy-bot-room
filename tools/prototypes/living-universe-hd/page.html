Living Universe HD — camera lab

Isolated prototype for PR #24. Placeholder art drawn in code; the real Pixel Pals floor, wander and crowd code moves the characters around a larger virtual room viewed through a camera. Not the app: no saved data, no AI, no private art.

Desktop: drag to pan, mouse wheel or trackpad pinch to zoom at the cursor, double-click to zoom in, click a character to follow it. Phone: one finger pans, two fingers pinch-zoom, tap a character to follow. Keyboard (focus the room): arrows or WASD, + and −, 0, F, Escape.
